There's a temptation in infrastructure to reach for the shiny thing — the newest
orchestrator, the cleverest abstraction, the framework everyone is tweeting about.

I've learned to resist it.

The platforms I'm proudest of are the ones nobody talks about, because they simply
work. No 2am pages. No "is the cluster down again?" in the team channel. Just a
quiet, dependable foundation that lets people build.

## Boring is a feature

- Boring is **predictable** — you can reason about it.
- Boring is **debuggable** — fewer moving parts, fewer mysteries.
- Boring is **onboardable** — new teammates get productive fast.

That doesn't mean never adopting new tools. It means earning complexity, not
defaulting to it. Every new dependency is a promise to maintain it.
